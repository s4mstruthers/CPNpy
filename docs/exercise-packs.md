# Writing exercise packs

The guide to writing exercise packs — folders of worksheets with answer boxes
that OpenProcess checks — ships with the app (*Help ▸ Writing Exercise Packs*) and
lives in [`openprocess/learn/exercise-packs.md`](../openprocess/learn/exercise-packs.md).
The demo pack in [`openprocess/exercises`](../openprocess/exercises) is a worked example of
every kind of answer box.

Check a pack before sharing it:

```
openprocess exercises check "path/to/pack" --answers
```
