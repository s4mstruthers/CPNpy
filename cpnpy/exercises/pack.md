# CPNpy demo exercises

Four short worksheets on Petri nets, soundness, the α-algorithm and regions.
Answer in the boxes and press **Check**: most answers are checked straight
away, and the app never shows you a result you are meant to work out until you
ask for it.

Your answers are saved in each exercise's folder (*my answers.json*, and
*my answer.pnml* for a net you draw), so you can stop and carry on later.

To write your own pack, copy this folder and change the `question.md` files:
see *Help ▸ Writing Exercise Packs*.
