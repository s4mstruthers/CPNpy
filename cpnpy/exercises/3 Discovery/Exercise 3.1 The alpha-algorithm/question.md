# Exercise 3.1 — The α-algorithm

Consider the event log

$$L = [\langle a,b,c,e \rangle^4, \langle a,c,b,e \rangle, \langle a,d,e \rangle^2]$$

a. Give the footprint matrix of $L$.

b. Apply the α-algorithm: give $T_L$, $T_I$, $T_O$, $X_L$ and $Y_L$.

c. Draw the discovered net $\alpha(L)$ and say whether it can replay every
   trace of $L$.

The log is open in the sidebar. Its Footprint tab and the Discover tab are
hidden until you reveal them.
