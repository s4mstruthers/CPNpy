# Exercise 4.1 — Regions of a transition system

The transition system (open in the sidebar) has the transitions

$s_0 \xrightarrow{a} s_1$, $s_0 \xrightarrow{b} s_2$, $s_1 \xrightarrow{b} s_3$,
$s_2 \xrightarrow{a} s_3$, $s_3 \xrightarrow{c} s_4$, $s_3 \xrightarrow{d} s_5$,
$s_4 \xrightarrow{e} s_6$, $s_5 \xrightarrow{e} s_6$, with initial state $s_0$.

a. Is $\{s_1, s_3\}$ a region? Is $\{s_3, s_4\}$? Explain.

b. Give the minimal pre-regions of $c$ and of $e$.

c. Does **forward closure** hold? Does **state separation** hold? Is the
   transition system elementary?

d. Synthesise the Petri net from the minimal regions. Does its reachability
   graph equal the transition system?

Use *Is this a region?* on the page to test your own sets of states; the
region lists and verdicts are hidden until you reveal them.
