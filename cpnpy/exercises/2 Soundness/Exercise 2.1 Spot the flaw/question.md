# Exercise 2.1 — Spot the flaw

The net shows how an insurer handles a complaint: it is **register**ed, then
the customer is contacted either by **send letter** or by **call customer**,
and finally the complaint is **archive**d.

a. Is the net a WF-net?

b. Is it **sound**? Check each of the three conditions:
   (i) option to complete, (ii) proper completion, (iii) no dead transitions.

c. For every condition that fails, give a firing sequence that shows it.

d. Change the net so that it is sound and still models the description.
   Your changes are saved as *my answer.pnml*; the given net stays as it was.

The Analysis tab is hidden in this exercise: reveal a card when you want to
check one of your answers.
